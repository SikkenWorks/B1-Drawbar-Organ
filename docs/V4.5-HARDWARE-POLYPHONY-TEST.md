# B1 V4.5 — FM1 Hardware Polyphony Test

This is a real firmware candidate but **not a hardware-proven crackle fix**. Before flashing, save your current firmware/project data and confirm you can enter an independently working recovery/updater mode. Do not experiment on your only device if you cannot recover it.

Record the same settings and USB power source for V4.4 and V4.5 to make comparisons meaningful.

1. Start with a clean B3 / ORGAN preset, 8' drawbar, ROCK drive low, rotor off, scanner off, delay/reverb off. Hold 1 note for 10s, then 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 12 notes. For each, note whether crackling begins immediately, only at attacks, or after holding the chord.
2. Repeat with all nine drawbars at 8, including glissandi, fast 3-key stabs and staggered note-ons. Check that 91 shared wheel phases and key click remain consistent.
3. Turn Hammond percussion on and off with three simultaneous UPPER notes. If the noise changes dramatically, that points to percussion arithmetic/CPU peak demand rather than just polyphony count.
4. Switch from B3 to ROCK/LORD, first with DRIVE low and then high, with the rotor/Leslie, scanner C1, delay and reverb tested **one by one**. A disturbance only at high output level despite no DMA overruns suggests output-stage/saturation rather than solely CPU deadline misses.
5. Open AUDIO DIAG. Record its **audio overrun count**, **peak audio time in microseconds** and safe/relief status for 1, 2, 3, 6, 12-note cases. Peak timing is historical since boot, not live average CPU %. A single peak >~2,900 microseconds is near the 128-frame@44.1kHz deadline. Reports should include exact values plus whether the screen showed an overrun.
6. Check USB-MIDI alongside key presses, KEY ASSIGN LEDs, 12 named project save/load and natural effects tails on releases. Wait 1 minute at idle and trigger a note: no click or missing first note.
7. If a persistent noise appears with **zero DMA overruns**, capture direct recorded audio (WAV if possible), the selected mode, drawbar registration, DRIVE, gain and other settings. Audio clipping and DMA underruns require different fixes.

Do not switch into a diagnostic developer or forced-flash mode to bypass a failed identity or loader handshake. Verify source/target pairing before attempting a cross-firmware update.
